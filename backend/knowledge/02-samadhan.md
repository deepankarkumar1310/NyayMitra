## What SAMADHAN is

SAMADHAN is an online portal of the Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India, where workers can file industrial disputes and labour complaints, such as non-payment of wages, illegal termination or denial of dues, and track their status.

## How to file a complaint on SAMADHAN

1. Open samadhan.labour.gov.in on a phone or at a Common Service Centre (CSC).
2. Register using your mobile number and verify with the OTP.
3. Choose the type of dispute (for example wages or termination) and fill in employer details: name, address and your work location.
4. Describe the problem in simple words with dates and amounts. Upload proof such as wage slips or messages if you have them.
5. Submit and note down the complaint number. You can track the status on the portal.

The complaint is sent to the concerned conciliation officer, who may call both the worker and the employer to settle the dispute.
