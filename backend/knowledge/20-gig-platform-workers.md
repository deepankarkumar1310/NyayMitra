## Who they are

Gig and platform workers include delivery partners, cab and bike-taxi drivers, and others who get work through apps. The Code on Social Security, 2020 recognises gig and platform workers and provides for social security schemes for them.

## What to do

1. Register on e-Shram as a gig or platform worker. Registration is free.
2. Keep screenshots of your earnings, trips, incentives and any account blocking or deactivation messages.
3. Use the app's grievance or support channel first and keep the ticket number.
4. Some states have their own gig worker laws and welfare boards; check with the state Labour Department.

Call the e-Shram helpline 14434 for help.
