## Inter-state migrant workers

The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 covers inter-state migrant workers. It provides for a journey allowance for travel to the native place, and the same wages as local workers for the same work.

## Ration anywhere

Under One Nation One Ration Card, a worker with a National Food Security Act ration card can take ration from any fair price shop in India using Aadhaar authentication. The Mera Ration app helps find nearby ration shops.

## Keep your documents

Keep copies (photos on your phone) of Aadhaar, bank passbook, e-Shram card, ration card, and any job letter or ID card from the employer. These help in claiming wages and benefits.
