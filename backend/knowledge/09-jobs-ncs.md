## National Career Service (NCS)

The National Career Service portal of the Ministry of Labour & Employment lists jobs from employers across India. Job seekers can register for free, search jobs by location and skill, and get information about job fairs.

## Avoid job fraud

Never pay money to an agent to get a job. Check the employer's name and address before travelling to another state. Keep the job offer in writing.
