## What gratuity is

Gratuity is a lump-sum payment from the employer when a worker leaves a job after long service. It is generally payable after at least five years of continuous service, on resignation, retirement, or termination. The five-year condition does not apply in case of death or disablement. Under the Code on Social Security, 2020, fixed-term employees can get gratuity in proportion to the service they completed.

## How much

Gratuity is generally calculated as 15 days' wages for every completed year of service, based on the last drawn wages.

## How to claim

1. Apply to the employer in writing after leaving the job.
2. The employer must calculate and pay the gratuity within the time set by law.
3. If the employer refuses, approach the Controlling Authority (Labour Department) or file on SAMADHAN.
