## Employer's duty

Under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020, employers must keep the workplace safe and free from hazards, give safety equipment (such as helmets, gloves, harnesses and masks) free of cost, give training, and provide drinking water, toilets and first aid.

## What you can do

1. Ask for safety equipment before doing risky work, for example working at height or with chemicals.
2. Take photos of unsafe conditions if you can do so safely.
3. Report unsafe conditions to the supervisor, the Labour Office or the Factories Inspector.
4. If someone is hurt, get medical help first and call 112 in an emergency.
