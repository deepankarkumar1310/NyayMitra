## Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan (PM-SYM)

PM-SYM is a voluntary, contributory pension scheme for unorganised workers. After the age of 60, the worker gets a minimum assured pension of ₹3,000 per month.

## Who can join

Unorganised workers aged 18 to 40 years with a monthly income of ₹15,000 or less, who are not members of EPF, ESIC or NPS and are not income tax payers.

## How it works

The worker pays a small monthly contribution depending on the age of joining, and the central government pays an equal matching amount.

## How to join

Visit a Common Service Centre (CSC) with Aadhaar and a savings bank account (or Jan Dhan account) with IFSC. You can find details of the scheme on myScheme.
