## One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)

Under One Nation One Ration Card, a beneficiary with a ration card under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) can take their foodgrains from any fair price shop in India using Aadhaar biometric authentication. A migrant worker's family can take part of the ration at home while the worker takes the rest in the city.

## How to use it

1. Make sure your ration card is linked with Aadhaar.
2. Go to any fair price shop (ration shop) with an ePoS machine and give your ration card number or Aadhaar.
3. Use the Mera Ration app to find nearby ration shops and check your entitlement.

If a ration shop refuses, note the shop details and complain to the state food department helpline.
