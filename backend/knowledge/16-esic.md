## What ESIC is

The Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) gives medical care and cash benefits to covered employees and their families. It generally covers establishments with 10 or more employees, for employees earning up to the wage limit set by ESIC (currently ₹21,000 a month).

## Benefits

- Free medical treatment for the worker and family at ESIC hospitals and dispensaries.
- Sickness benefit: cash payment during certified sickness.
- Maternity benefit for women workers.
- Disablement benefit and dependants' benefit in case of employment injury.

## How to use it

1. Ask your employer for your ESIC insurance number (IP number) and e-Pehchan card.
2. Visit the nearest ESIC dispensary or hospital with your card.
3. If your employer cuts ESI from wages but you have no card, ask in writing and contact the nearest ESIC branch office.
