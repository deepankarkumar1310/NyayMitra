## myScheme

myScheme is a Government of India platform to search for central and state government schemes. A worker can answer a few questions (state, age, occupation, income) and see schemes they may be eligible for, with the documents needed and how to apply.

## Getting help applying

Common Service Centres (CSC) help people apply for government schemes and register on portals like e-Shram. The e-Shram helpline 14434 can also guide workers about schemes.
