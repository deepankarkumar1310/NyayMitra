## After losing a job

1. Collect all wages and dues from the employer (wages within two working days of removal).
2. If you were in EPF, check your PF balance; part of it can be withdrawn when unemployed under EPFO rules.
3. If you were covered by ESIC, check with ESIC whether you are eligible for unemployment allowance under its schemes.

## Finding new work and skills

- Search for jobs and job fairs on the National Career Service portal.
- Skill training courses under Skill India / PMKVY are available at training centres; ask at a CSC or check myScheme.
- Register on e-Shram so you can be linked to schemes (helpline 14434).
