## The law

Under the Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986, children below 14 years cannot be employed in any occupation or process (except helping family after school hours in certain non-hazardous cases). Adolescents aged 14 to 18 cannot be employed in hazardous occupations and processes.

## How to report

1. Report child labour on the PENCIL portal (Platform for Effective Enforcement for No Child Labour).
2. Call the child helpline 1098 for a child in danger or need.
3. In an emergency, call 112.
