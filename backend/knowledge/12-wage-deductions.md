## Only allowed deductions

Under the Code on Wages, 2019, an employer can deduct money from wages only for reasons allowed by law, such as income tax, provident fund or ESI contributions, recovery of advances or loans, fines allowed by law, absence from duty, or damage or loss caused by the worker's neglect.

The total of all deductions in a wage period should not be more than 50% of the wages.

## Things that are not allowed

- Cutting wages without telling the worker the reason.
- Taking back the cost of tools, uniforms or safety equipment without a legal basis.
- Holding back wages to stop a worker from leaving.

If money is cut unfairly, ask for the reason in writing, keep your wage slips and bank records, and complain on SAMADHAN or at the Labour Office.
