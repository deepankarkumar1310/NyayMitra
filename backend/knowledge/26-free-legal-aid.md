## Free legal aid

Under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, free legal services are available to many groups, including women, children, members of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, industrial workmen, victims of trafficking, persons with disabilities, people in custody, and people with low income.

Free legal aid can include a free lawyer, help with drafting complaints and applications, and advice.

## How to get it

1. Call the NALSA legal aid helpline 15100.
2. Visit the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) office at your district court.
3. Lok Adalats also help settle disputes quickly and without court fees.
