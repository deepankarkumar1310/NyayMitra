## First steps after an injury

1. Get medical treatment first. In an emergency call 112.
2. Tell the employer or supervisor about the accident as soon as possible, preferably in writing.
3. Keep all hospital papers, bills, medical certificates and photos of the injury and the place of the accident.
4. Write down names and phone numbers of co-workers who saw the accident.
5. For a serious accident, a police report (FIR) may be needed.

## Compensation

Employers are liable to pay compensation to a worker injured by an accident arising out of and in the course of employment, under the Employee's Compensation Act, 1923 and the Code on Social Security, 2020. If the worker is covered by ESIC, benefits are claimed through ESIC.

Construction workers registered with their state Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board may get extra help from the board.

If the employer refuses, contact the local Labour Office or file a complaint on SAMADHAN. Call 14434 for guidance.
