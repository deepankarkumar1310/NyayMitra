## Your rights when you are removed

If you are removed from work, ask the employer for the reason in writing. All wages due must be paid within two working days of removal.

Workers who have completed at least one year of continuous service and are retrenched (removed for reasons other than punishment) are generally entitled to notice or wages in place of notice, and retrenchment compensation of 15 days' average pay for every completed year of service. Gratuity may also be due if the worker meets the eligibility conditions.

## What to do

1. Do not sign any paper (resignation, full and final settlement) that you do not understand.
2. Collect proof that you worked there: ID card, attendance photos, wage slips, bank entries, appointment letter.
3. Ask in writing for your pending wages and dues.
4. If the employer refuses, file a complaint on the SAMADHAN portal or go to the local Labour Office.
5. Call the e-Shram helpline 14434 for guidance.
