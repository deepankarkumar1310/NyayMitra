## Warning signs of a fake job

- You are asked to pay money for a job, training, uniform or "registration".
- The salary promised is much higher than normal.
- No written offer, no company address, or only a WhatsApp number.
- The agent asks to keep your Aadhaar, passport or phone.

## If you were cheated online

1. Call the cyber crime helpline 1930 immediately, especially if money was transferred - quick reporting can help stop the money.
2. Report on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
3. Keep screenshots of chats, payment receipts and phone numbers.

## Trafficking

If someone is being taken somewhere against their will or held and forced to work, call 112. For children, call 1098.
