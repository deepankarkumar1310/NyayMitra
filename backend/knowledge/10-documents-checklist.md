## What a wage slip or appointment letter should show

A proper appointment letter or wage slip usually shows: the employer's name and address, the worker's name and designation, the date of joining, the wage rate and pay period, days worked, overtime, deductions with reasons, and the net amount paid.

## Things to watch for

- No employer name, stamp or signature.
- Wage lower than what was promised or lower than the minimum wage for the state and job.
- Deductions without a reason.
- Missing dates or pay period.
- The worker's name spelled wrongly or a different person's details.

Under the Code on Wages, 2019, employers must issue wage slips to employees. Keep every slip and match it with your bank entries.
