## Bonded labour is illegal

The Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976 abolishes bonded labour in India. Forcing someone to work to repay a loan or advance, not letting them leave, or not paying them is a crime. Any such debt is cancelled by law.

## Signs of bonded labour

- You cannot leave the workplace or are locked in.
- The employer or agent keeps your Aadhaar, phone or documents.
- You must work without pay to repay an advance.
- You are threatened or beaten.

## Get help

1. In danger, call 112 immediately.
2. Inform the District Magistrate (DM) or Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) - they are responsible for freeing and rehabilitating bonded labourers.
3. Released bonded labourers are entitled to a release certificate and rehabilitation assistance under the central scheme.
4. Free legal help is available from the Legal Services Authority (NALSA helpline 15100).
