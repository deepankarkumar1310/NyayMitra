## The law

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 protects women at all workplaces, including domestic workers and informal workers.

## Where to complain

1. If the workplace has 10 or more workers, complain to the Internal Committee (IC) of the employer.
2. If there is no Internal Committee, or the complaint is against the employer, or the woman is a domestic worker, complain to the Local Committee set up by the District Officer.
3. A complaint should normally be made within three months of the incident.
4. Women can also file a complaint online on the SHe-Box portal of the Ministry of Women and Child Development.

## Urgent help

If you are in danger, call 112. The women helpline number is 181.
