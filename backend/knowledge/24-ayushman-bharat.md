## What PM-JAY gives

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) gives eligible families health cover of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for hospital treatment at empanelled government and private hospitals. Treatment is cashless.

## Check if you are eligible

1. Check eligibility on the PM-JAY website or the beneficiary portal with your mobile number or ration card.
2. Ask the Ayushman Mitra at an empanelled hospital.
3. Call the PM-JAY helpline 14555.

## Ayushman card

Eligible people can get an Ayushman card made at a Common Service Centre, an empanelled hospital, or through the official app, using Aadhaar.
