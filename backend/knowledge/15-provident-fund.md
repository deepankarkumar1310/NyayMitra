## What EPF is

The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), run by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), is a savings fund for workers. It generally applies to establishments with 20 or more employees. Both the employee and the employer contribute a share of wages every month (usually 12% each of basic wage plus dearness allowance).

## Your UAN

Every EPF member gets a Universal Account Number (UAN). The UAN stays the same when you change jobs. Activate it on the EPFO member portal using your mobile number and Aadhaar.

## Checking and withdrawing

1. Check your PF balance and passbook on the EPFO member portal or the UMANG app.
2. Make sure your Aadhaar, bank account and mobile number are linked to your UAN (KYC).
3. Claims for withdrawal or transfer can be filed online on the member portal.

## If the employer deducts PF but does not deposit it

If PF is cut from your salary but does not appear in your passbook, keep your wage slips and file a grievance on the EPFO grievance portal (EPFiGMS) or visit the nearest EPFO office.
