## Where to complain

You can complain to the Labour Office in the district where you work (Inspector-cum-Facilitator / Labour Enforcement Officer), or online on SAMADHAN. For central government establishments, mines, railways, ports and banks, the Chief Labour Commissioner (Central) offices handle complaints.

## What to write

A simple complaint should include:
1. Your name, address and phone number.
2. The employer's or contractor's name and address, and the place of work.
3. Your job, date of joining, and agreed wage.
4. What happened: unpaid wages for which months, the amount, or the date you were removed.
5. What you want: payment of wages, dues, or reinstatement.
6. A list of proofs attached (wage slips, attendance photos, messages, bank entries).

Keep a copy and ask for a receipt or complaint number.
