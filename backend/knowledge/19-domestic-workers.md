## Protection for domestic workers

Domestic workers (house cleaning, cooking, child care, elderly care) are unorganised workers. They can register on e-Shram for free and become eligible for social security schemes. Many states have also included domestic work in their minimum wage notifications.

## Harassment

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013 covers domestic workers. A domestic worker can complain to the Local Committee in the district, or use the SHe-Box portal.

## Safety tips

- Keep the employer's name, address and phone number with a family member.
- Never give your Aadhaar card or phone to the employer or agent to keep.
- Placement agencies should not take your wages. Keep a record of what was agreed.
- In danger, call 112. Women helpline: 181.
