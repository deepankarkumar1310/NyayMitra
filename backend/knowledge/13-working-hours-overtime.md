## Working hours

Labour laws limit normal working hours. As a general rule, a worker should not work more than 8 hours a day and 48 hours a week, with rest intervals and a weekly day of rest. Exact rules can differ for some industries and states.

## Overtime

Work beyond the normal hours is overtime. Under the labour codes, overtime must be paid at twice the ordinary rate of wages. Overtime should be recorded.

## What to do

1. Note down your daily start and end time (a photo or note on your phone each day is enough).
2. Keep wage slips to see whether overtime was paid.
3. If overtime is not paid, ask in writing and then complain on SAMADHAN or at the Labour Office.
