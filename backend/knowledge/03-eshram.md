## What e-Shram is

e-Shram is the national database of unorganised workers created by the Ministry of Labour & Employment. Migrant workers, construction workers, domestic workers, gig and platform workers, street vendors and agricultural workers can register. Registration is free.

## Who can register

A person aged 16 to 59 years who works in the unorganised sector, is not a member of EPFO or ESIC, and does not pay income tax can register.

## How to register

1. Keep your Aadhaar number, the mobile number linked to Aadhaar, and your bank account details ready.
2. Self-register on eshram.gov.in using the Aadhaar-linked mobile number and OTP, or visit a nearby Common Service Centre (CSC) for free help.
3. After registration you get an e-Shram card with a Universal Account Number (UAN).

## Benefits

Registered workers can be linked to social security and welfare schemes. Accident insurance under Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) has been offered to registered workers.

## Helpline

The e-Shram helpline number is 14434. Call it for help with registration and to know about schemes.
