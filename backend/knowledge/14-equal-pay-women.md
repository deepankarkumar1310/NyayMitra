## Equal pay

The Code on Wages, 2019 forbids discrimination on the basis of gender in wages and in recruitment for the same work or work of a similar nature. A woman doing the same work as a man must get the same wage.

## Night work

Under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020, women may work at night only with their consent and with safety, holidays and working-hours conditions that the government sets.

## Maternity benefit

Women workers who meet the eligibility conditions are entitled to paid maternity leave. For the first two children, maternity benefit is up to 26 weeks. Establishments covered by ESIC pay maternity benefit through ESIC. An employer cannot dismiss a woman because of her pregnancy or during maternity leave.

If you face unequal pay or are removed due to pregnancy, keep proof and complain on SAMADHAN or at the Labour Office. For harassment, see the SHe-Box portal. Women helpline: 181.
