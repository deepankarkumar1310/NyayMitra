## What the law says

Under the Code on Wages, 2019, every employer must pay at least the minimum wage fixed by the government for that kind of work and that area. The central government fixes a floor wage, and state governments cannot fix minimum wages below this floor.

Minimum wages are different in each state and depend on the type of work and skill level (unskilled, semi-skilled, skilled, highly skilled). They are revised from time to time and usually include a variable dearness allowance (VDA).

## How to check your minimum wage

1. Check the website of your state Labour Department for the current minimum wage notification.
2. For central sphere work (railways, mines, central government contracts, etc.) check the Chief Labour Commissioner (Central) notifications.
3. Ask at a Common Service Centre (CSC) or call the e-Shram helpline 14434.

## If you are paid less

Keep proof of the wage you received (slips, bank entries, messages). You can complain to the Labour Office (Inspector-cum-Facilitator) or file on the SAMADHAN portal. A claim for the difference can be made for the unpaid amount.
