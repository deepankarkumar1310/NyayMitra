## Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) welfare boards

Every state has a Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board. It is funded by a cess collected on construction projects. Registered construction workers can get benefits such as accident assistance, help for education of children, maternity help, pension and housing support. Benefits differ by state.

## Who can register

Generally, a construction worker aged 18 to 60 who has worked in building or construction work for at least 90 days in the last 12 months can register with the state board.

## How to register

1. Get a certificate of 90 days of work from the employer, contractor, or a registered trade union, as your state requires.
2. Apply at the state board office, through the state's online portal, or at a Common Service Centre.
3. Keep your registration card and renew it as required.

Also register on e-Shram (helpline 14434).
