## When wages must be paid

Under the Code on Wages, 2019, wages must be paid on time. Daily-rated workers are paid at the end of the shift. Weekly-rated workers are paid on the last working day of the week. Fortnightly-rated workers are paid before the end of the second day after the fortnight ends. Monthly-rated workers are paid before the seventh day of the next month.

If a worker is removed, retrenched, dismissed or resigns, the wages due must be paid within two working days.

## What a worker can do about unpaid wages

1. Write down the dates you worked, the agreed wage and how much is unpaid.
2. Keep proof: wage slips, attendance register photos, bank statements, UPI messages, appointment letter, and names of co-workers who can confirm.
3. Ask the employer or contractor for payment in writing (a message or letter) and keep a copy.
4. If not paid, file a complaint online on the SAMADHAN portal of the Ministry of Labour & Employment, or visit the local Labour Office (Inspector-cum-Facilitator).
5. Call the e-Shram helpline 14434 for guidance.

## Contractor workers

If you were hired through a contractor and the contractor does not pay, the principal employer (the company where the work was done) can be responsible for making sure wages are paid. Mention both the contractor and the principal employer in your complaint.
